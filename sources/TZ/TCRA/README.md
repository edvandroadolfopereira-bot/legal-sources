Legal and regulatory documents from the Tanzania Communications Regulatory
Authority (TCRA), the converged regulator for electronic and postal
communications and broadcasting in Tanzania.

## Coverage

Documents are pulled from the TCRA document library at
`tcra.go.tz/documents/{category}` across these categories:

- **legislation / legislation-1** — Acts (e.g. EPOCA, TCRA Act, Cybercrimes Act)
  → `legislation`
- **regulations** — sector regulations → `legislation`
- **rules** — rules of procedure → `legislation`
- **codes** — codes of conduct → `doctrine`
- **guidelines** — regulatory guidelines → `doctrine`

Each document card exposes a URL-encoded `/download/` link to a text-based PDF.
Full text is extracted with pdfplumber; scanned/image-only PDFs (no extractable
text) are skipped.

=======
Regulatory documents from the Tanzania Communications Regulatory Authority (TCRA),
the independent ICT and postal regulator.

## Coverage

- **Legislation (Sheria)** — TCRA Act, EPOCA, Cybercrimes Act, Finance Acts, etc.
- **Policy (Sera)** — National ICT Policy, Postal Policy, Telecommunications Policy
- **Regulations (Kanuni)** — 46+ subsidiary regulations under EPOCA
- **Rules** — Licensing procedure rules, inquiry rules, community broadcasting rules
- **Guidelines (Miongozo)** — Satellite, roaming, SLA, device detection, type approval
- **Codes** — Electoral advertising code for political parties
- **Sector plans** — Numbering plans, spectrum strategy, postcode allocation

Documents are fetched as PDFs from `tcra.go.tz/download/` and full text extracted
via pdfplumber. Scanned/image-only PDFs are skipped.
